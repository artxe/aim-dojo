# calc/

Numeric sens/CPI/mouse-scale math. DOM lives in `controller/`, FOV side effects in `logic.js`.

## Files
Two files, not three — `calc_pubg.js` is GONE (`calc_pubg_converted()` moved into `calc_sens.js`; its dead `state_free_files` entry was dropped from `eslint.config.js` in 2026-08).
- `calc_sens.js` — per-game in-engine sens + `calc_rad_per_px()` + `calc_real_hfov()` + `calc_sens_snap()` + `calc_sens_cs2_yaw()` + `calc_sens_r6_file()` + `calc_sens_rb_fields()` + `calc_pubg_converted()` + `lol_sens_to_cpi_scale()`. No CPI Y/X here — it is the literal `constants.cpi.ratio` (`controller/CLAUDE.md`). Imports `../math.js` + `../constants.js` ONLY. `logic.js` pulls `calc_real_hfov` + `calc_sens_snap` (+ `calc_sens_cs2`/`calc_sens_rb`/`calc_sens_val` for the gap between the field the player typed and the view a run renders), so renderer and card share one aspect implementation and one rounding model
- `calc_cpi.js` — per-game CPI that preserves turning; reads `state.cpi_norm`, takes zero params, each fn opens with a dev-guard throw if `state.cpi_norm.game` isn't its own. Every fn divides `state.game.width`/`height` by `state.game.cpi_scale` — the SAME basis the card and the three settings-file rewrites use, so a LOL in-game sens ≠ 50 can't drift the normalizer off the displayed sens

## Two layers
`calc_cpi_*` (main thread) read from `state`, take ZERO params. `calc_sens_*` / `calc_rad_per_px` are PURE and MUST stay parameterized (`fov`/`width`/`height`, plus `cpi_scale` for `bdo` alone) — never make them read `state` to "reduce params". **`state.js` imports `lol_sens_to_cpi_scale` from here, so `calc_sens.js` importing `state.js` is a cycle** — that, not the worker, pins the boundary now (the old `calc_worker.js` reason expired 2026-08 when the card moved main-thread; the constraint did not).

Lint-enforced by a file-scoped `no-restricted-syntax` allow-list in `eslint.config.js` (NOT an inline header): `calc_sens → math/constants`, `constants.js → math.js` only, `math.js` → nothing. Same guard on `bg_worker.js` and `writing_worker.js` (`../constants.js` + `../math.js`).

## Non-obvious
- `calc_rad_per_px(fov_deg, width)` → rad/px. Match point is derived INTERNALLY from FOV geometry: `match = width * (1 - cos(fov/2)) / 2`, the sagitta (arc-height in px) of the FOV arc with `width` as diameter — NOT arc-height ÷ diameter. So matching distance grows super-linearly with FOV and collapses to 0 (plain linear sens) as FOV → 0. Because `match ∝ width`, `width` cancels and the result is exactly ∝ `1/width` — **which is WHY `match` must stay a length (px), not the dimensionless ratio**. Returns the mean rad/px over `[0, match]` (secant slope), slightly below the on-center peak (~3 % at 90°, ~18 % at 120°). Hot inner of nearly every sens calc; `on_mousemove` does NOT call it — it reads the cached `state.camera.sens`
- No `match`/CPI parameter. CPI affects sens ONLY via (a) OS pixels feeding cached `state.camera.sens` and (b) the explicit `cpi * sens` factor inside `calc_cpi_*`; the match point is purely geometric (FOV + width), identical across CPIs
- **Sens is VIEW-INDEPENDENT** — FPP/TPP is render-only. `state.camera.sens` is `calc_rad_per_px(fov, width)` regardless of dimension (× `sens_scale`, which is rounding + SA's forced zoom + the typed-field-vs-shown-view gap, never a DIMENSION term). TPP feel is re-created visually by the `state.camera.dist` orbit, so parallax exists identically in trainer and game and cancels out of every sens number — yaw-per-count is the whole match, and `constants.tpp.*` is renderer-ONLY. Feel-verified three times (pubg TPP-hipfire ↔ ADS transition naturally; bdo's old ×2.88 read ~133 @FHD when plain ≈46 was right; mc's old ×~1.79 read "way too slow"), then removed 2026-07 with `calc_tpp_rad_per_px`/`calc_sens_fn_tpp`/`calc_sens_mc_tpp`/`calc_pubg_fpp_fov`/`constants.tpp.parallax_distance_scale`. **Never reintroduce a parallax/cross-view multiplier**
- **Every `calc_sens_*` uses its game's FOV in TWO senses — conflating them was the pre-2026-08 bug:**
  - NUMERATOR `calc_rad_per_px(hfov, width)` = monitor-match GEOMETRY: MUST be the true horizontal at the REAL aspect, so it always goes through one `convert_deg_across_aspect` from the declared basis
  - DENOMINATOR = the ENGINE's yaw-per-count rule in the game's OWN fov units at its OWN reference basis (`hfov/base` for `cs2`/`val`/`pubg`, tan-ratio for `al`/`fn`/`r6`, bare constant for `ow`/`rb`). Aspect-INVARIANT, stays on the RAW argument
- **`calc_real_hfov(axis, fov_deg, width, height)` owns the numerator rebasing** — the three `FovAxis` rules live here and nowhere else. ONE exception to keep in step: `render/camera.js:calc_camera_sens`'s `bdo`/`sa` hipfire locks re-derive the same expressions inline (`camera.js` imports `calc_rad_per_px` but not `calc_real_hfov`). Policy table in root `CLAUDE.md`. Every `calc_sens_*` reads `constants.fov.<game>.axis` and delegates, so adding a game means declaring an `axis`, not writing a formula:
  - `"h"` (`fn`, `ow` hipfire, `pubg`, `val`): returns RAW — already the real horizontal at every aspect. A `16/9` rebase here is a REGRESSION (tried, reverted 2026-08); it hands ultrawide a side-FOV bonus these games do not give
  - `"v"` (`bdo`, `mc`, `r6`, `rb`, `ow` scopes, plus `lol` — renderer-only, no sens calc): `convert_deg_across_aspect(vfov, height, width)`
  - `"43"` (`al`, `cs2`, `sa`): `convert_deg_across_aspect(hfov, 4/3, max(4/3, width/height))`. The `max` clamps the EFFECTIVE aspect at 4:3 — that IS the crossover: wider than 4:3 the vertical locks and the horizontal widens (cs2 90 → 106.26° @16:9 → 121.66° @21:9); narrower/taller the horizontal locks at the declared number (cs2 stays 90° at 5:4 and 4:3). Always test BOTH sides of 4:3
- **`ow` is the one game whose two FOV families lock different axes**, so `calc_sens_ow` cannot delegate for both — it takes the TRUE horizontal and the CALLER supplies it. `ow.hipfire` (103, `"h"`) and `ow.scopes.widow`/`ashe`/`emre`/`freja` (RAW in-game VERTICAL under `scopes.axis` = `"v"`, the only nested axis in `constants.fov`) each go through `calc_real_hfov` at the `controller/game_sens.js` call site. Safe because its divisor is a bare `.006_6` with no fov term. Consequence: the widow multiplier MOVES with aspect (30.43 % @4:3, 40.55 % @16:9, 54.35 % @21:9) — correct, hipfire is pinned while the scope widens
- `sa` was a pillarbox case (`height * 4 / 3` crop box, 85° verbatim) until 2026-08, when the crop and its `calc_camera_sens()` branch were removed on request. Now in the 4:3 group with `al`/`cs2`: identical to the old formula on a true 4:3 monitor, ~6.6 % lower @16:9, ~26 % lower @21:9
- `convert_deg_across_aspect` doubles as a fixed-ratio ZOOM when both spans are constants. All FOV values live in `constants.js`'s `fov.<game>` IIFEs, computed once at module load: `val`'s 2.5× ADS is `convert_deg_across_aspect(hipfire, 2.5, 1)` (same for `al`/`r6` `xN`); `ow`'s hero FOVs stay RAW vertical, rebased at the call site. `logic.update_camera_view()` and `controller/game_sens.js` just read `constants.fov.<game>.<field>`. Constant-span calls are aspect-INDEPENDENT, so they never go stale on resize
- `calc_sens_*` returns raw in-engine sens; `controller/game_sens.js:update_game_sens()` derives multipliers (`x2`, `x4`, scope) by re-calling at the zoomed FOV over the hipfire baseline. Zoomed fields are usually `convert_deg_across_aspect(base, N, 1)`, NOT a literal `FOV/N` — only `pubg` divides directly (`base/N`), and `cs2` stores literal scope FOVs (`aug` 45, `auto2` 15, `awp2` 10). **Three `pubg` fields are deliberately NOT `base/N` and must not be "fixed"**: `x4` = `base / 4 - 1`; `x1` = `base - 5` (the red-dot/holo optic barely zooms — 75° against an 80° reference, and it is the value PUBG wants in BOTH `Targeting` and `Scoping`); `x15` = `base / 12`, because the scope the game calls 15x is really a **6-12x adjusted with the mouse wheel** and one `Scope15X` sensitivity covers the whole range, so the card matches its MAXIMUM — the same end `x6`/`x8` are stored at
- **`fn` has ONE formula**: `calc_sens_fn` (tan-ratio) IS Zoom Scaling **Standard**, which applies that ratio for the player — so the card pins `ADS`/`Scoped` at a literal 100 % and only the hipfire percent is typed. The linear fov-ratio `calc_sens_fn_legacy` was the **Legacy** setting and is GONE (2026-08); bringing it back means bringing back two typed percent fields and their `mul_decimals` grid

### No vertical fields (decision 2026-09-05)
Five games expose a vertical sensitivity of their own (`fn` Y-Axis, `cs2` m_pitch, `pubg` Vertical, `r6` Vertical, `rb` Vertical) and the card prints every one of them EQUAL to its horizontal twin: `fn_y_el` ← `fn_hipfire`, `cs2_pitch_el` ← `cs2_yaw`, `r6_vertical_el` ← `r6_yaw`, `rb_vertical_el` ← `rb_horizontal`, PUBG a static `1` in the markup and `1.000000` in the INI rewrite. The vertical axis is the MOUSE's job alone (`constants.cpi.ratio` = 1.5, `controller/CLAUDE.md`): nothing in `calc/` takes a `cpi_v`, `constants.fov.<game>` has no `vertical_ref`, `state.camera` has no `pitch_scale`/`sens_y`, and `window.js` moves pitch by the same `sens` as yaw. For four days of 2026-09 each field was matched at a per-game `vertical_ref` view through a `calc_vh_ratio()` (the view's vertical/horizontal optimum) divided by the mouse's `cpi_v = CPI-Y / (√2 × CPI-X)`, and the trainer reproduced the ratio as `pitch_scale` — all removed with the per-game CPI ratio, because a vertical matched per view IS a per-FOV vertical, exactly what the fixed 1.5 rejects. Do not print a vertical field that differs from the horizontal without reopening that decision.
- `cs2`: `calc_sens_cs2_yaw()` — `m_yaw = .022 × X_exact / X_typed` at `yaw_decimals` 6 absorbs the 2 dp rounding of the `sensitivity` field, so the hipfire snap is ≈ 1 (it read −0.090 % with `m_yaw` pinned at .022) and every zoom multiplier on the card divides by that exact base (`cs2_base = typed × m_yaw / .022`), not the typed number; `m_pitch` prints the same number
- `r6`: inside `calc_sens_r6_file(width, height)` — the in-game `Horizontal`/`Vertical` sliders are INTEGERS 1–`slider_max` (100) on top of the 6 dp `MouseSensitivityMultiplierUnit`, so `yaw` is searched: for every `yaw` the unit is `round6(hipfire × .02 / yaw)` (a bigger yaw makes the unit smaller and its 6 dp rounding coarser) and the yaw whose rounding error is smallest wins (100 evaluations, first of a tie — yaw 1 @FHD, where the unit is ≈ .30 and 6 dp barely rounds, so the card reads `1, 0.30088`; memoised on `(width, height)`); `Vertical` prints that same `yaw`. Both sliders and the unit go to the INI; the ADS ints are multipliers on top and do not move with `yaw`. `calc_sens_snap`'s `r6` branch reads `yaw × yaw_unit` off the file, no longer `round6(hipfire × .02)` alone

### rb (the only FOV-INDEPENDENT yaw)
Roblox rotates `.5°` per count at `MouseSensitivity` 1 whatever the FOV — that is `calc_sens_rb`'s whole divisor. Rivals breaks it ONLY while aiming: its zoom sens at **100 % is ALREADY scaled to `ads_vfov / constants.fov.rb.base`** (`base` = 120, the in-game slider), a plain linear FOV reduction. `update_game_sens()` therefore divides by that same `vfov / rb.base` scale to undo it, then by `rb_base_sens` to land on the PERCENT the `While Aiming`/`While Scoped` fields take. The five settable values come from `calc_sens_rb_fields(width, height)` (below); `update_game_sens()` only reads them and derives the per-weapon reference rows.
- `rb_hipfire` = `constants.fov.rb.rivals.hipfire` (70, Rivals' hipfire FOV), **NOT** `rb.base` (120, Roblox's slider and the rendered view) — the `rivals` nesting exists to keep those apart
- **Rivals has THREE hipfire fields and they MULTIPLY: `Sensitivity`, `Horizontal`, `Vertical` (each 5–400 % in 5 % steps), then `While Aiming`/`While Scoped` (5–200 %) on top, applied to BOTH axes.** The horizontal chain is `Roblox × Sensitivity × Horizontal`, the vertical chain swaps `Vertical` in for `Horizontal`, and the exact rebasing of the hipfire chain would be `calc_sens_rb(base) / rb_hipfire` (140.6 % @FHD). **Order of operations (2026-09): `While Aiming` is snapped FIRST** — `aiming_exact` on an exactly rebased base (119.13 % @FHD → 120; a local of the search, the card never prints it), because that is the number the player sees — **then `Sensitivity × Horizontal` is tuned so that, multiplied by that ROUNDED aiming percent, the rifle's 45° view is exact** (`target_ads = base_exact × aiming_exact / aiming`), and `Vertical` prints the same number as `Horizontal` (no vertical fields, above). Every field sits on a 5 % grid, so `calc_sens_rb_fields()` runs a grid search — 80 × 80 `Sensitivity`/`Horizontal` pairs, ~0.4 ms, memoised on `(width, height)` like `calc_sens_r6_file` — keeping the pair whose horizontal chain lands closest to `target_ads`. Hipfire is NOT in the objective; it is a consequence, and the card prints it: `Horizontal`'s parentheses read `(hipfire-exact / aiming-exact)`, the value the field would need for the two views at the chosen `Sensitivity`. Ties (many pairs share a product: 80 × 175 = 140 × 100) go to the `Horizontal` nearest 100. `effective` = `Roblox × Sensitivity × Horizontal / 10_000` is what the player ACTUALLY ends up with at the 120° view (@FHD 155 × 90 = 139.5 %, −0.78 % against the hipfire rebasing but −0.06 % against the aiming target), and every weapon row is `calc_sens_rb(fov) × base × 100 / (effective × fov)`
- Weapon rows land ABOVE 100 % because `calc_rad_per_px`'s sagitta match falls off slower than linear FOV (45° needs ≈ .447 of the 120° yaw; linear gives `.375`)
- **The settable fields are `Sensitivity` / `Horizontal` / `Vertical` (= `Horizontal`) / `While Aiming` (= `While Scoped`)** — `Horizontal` prints `(hipfire-exact / aiming-exact)` at the chosen `Sensitivity` (`Horizontal: 175% (175.75 / 175.21)`), `While Aiming` (and `While Scoped`, and the Assault Rifle/Sniper reference row — all three are `rb_rifle_exact`) prints the value it would need for ZERO error on the CHOSEN `Sensitivity × Horizontal`, `calc_sens_rb(rifle) / rb_base_sens / rifle` like every other weapon row (`120% (120.06)` @FHD) — ≈ the rounded number by construction, since the pair was tuned against it, and its 5 % rounding is always the printed `aiming` because the pair's residual (≤ a few tenths of a percent) is far below half a step. Until 2026-09-05 it printed `aiming_exact` on the rebased base (119.13), a basis no printed field sits on and one the `Horizontal` parentheses do not use. `While Aiming` is pinned to `rivals.rifle` (45), the fov `constants.fov.rb.ads_ref` names for EVERY weapon, because Rivals gives them all one box. Having it follow the card's `Trainer ADS` selector instead (tried 2026-08) printed a matched number for whatever was picked and so HID the error that single box actually leaves: @FHD the Minigun runs **+13.73 %** on the rifle-matched 120. The six per-weapon rows stay reference readouts (`_exact` only)
- **How far the six spread is an ASPECT fact, not a resolution one** — snapped: @16:9 120/120/120/120/110/105, @21:9 150/150/140/125/110/105, @5:4 90/95/95/100/100/100. So @FHD the reference decides only two rows (the grid puts 45/60/75/90 all on 120; only `slingshot` 110 and `minigun` 105 differ). Resolution nudges ONE row across a step (@4K/16:9 `shotgun` → 115) unless it moves the `Sensitivity × Horizontal` product itself, which re-bases `effective` and shifts every row at once (2560x1080 reads 120, 3440x1440 reads 115). Unsnapped @FHD: 45° 119.63 %, 60° 122.10 %, 75° 122.13 %, 90° 118.13 %, 105° 110.32 %, 112.5° 105.52 % — the 5 % grid costs +0.31 % (rifle) to −1.74 % (exogun)

### r6
- `calc_sens_r6_file()` produces the `GameSettings.ini` values AND the card: a searched integer yaw (no vertical fields, above; `Vertical` prints the same yaw) with the 6 dp `MouseSensitivityMultiplierUnit` carrying the rest of the hipfire sens; 8 ADS scope ints ≤ 200 share a 6 dp `ADSMouseMultiplierUnit` grid-scanned over `[max_ratio/200, max_ratio/100]` for minimum worst-scope error
- **It memoises its last `(width, height)`.** That grid scan is ~100 k iterations (0.21 ms) and `calc_sens_snap("r6", ads_fov)` puts it on the RMB-down path, where it re-ran every ADS entry at the same resolution; cached call is **0.0015 ms**. Returns the SAME object on a hit → treat as read-only (every caller destructures). Resolution change invalidates. Also makes repeat `update_game_sens()` near-free — the scan was ~100 % of its 0.181 ms

## constants.sens
**Every per-game engine coefficient lives in `constants.sens.<game>`, NOT in the formula** — each was previously typed out in 2–4 files and that is exactly how they drift. Copy from the game's actual config; don't guess.
- LINEAR — `scale`: `al`/`cs2` `.022`, `fn` `.005_555`, `ow` `.006_6`, `r6` `.005_73`, `rb` `.5`, `val` `.07`
- AFFINE in raw rad — `step * s + offset`: `bdo` (`.000_023_75` / `.000_125`), `sa` (`.000_03` / `.000_45`). Their `calc_cpi_*` inverts the affine form, in the SAME units `calc_sens_*` uses — raw rad, no `to_rad` wrapper (`calc_cpi_sa` carried one until 2026-08 and returned `cpi × π/180`; the check is that feeding a card's displayed sens back into the normalizer at the same CPI returns that CPI)
- CUBIC — `mc`: `to_rad(base) * (offset + step * s) ** 3`
- LOG — `pubg`: `base_sens` 50, `step` ≈ 15.0515, plus `base_yaw` and the `converted_base` `.02` `calc_pubg_converted` needs

**`decimals` is the input grid, load-bearing in TWO directions**: `controller/game_sens.js` rounds the displayed hipfire to it and `calc_sens_snap` rounds to it again to build the trainer's ratio — if they disagreed, the trainer would run at a sens the card never showed, the exact bug `calc_sens_snap` prevents. One constant makes that impossible.
- `bdo`/`sa` use `decimals: 0` because **their in-game fields are INTEGERS** — that coarse grid is why they carry the largest snap error (+0.315 % / +0.289 % @FHD). `round_to(x, 0)` verified identical to `round(x)` across 45 504 real sens values + `toFixed` half-way cases
- `mc` uses `decimals: 16` because **the real MC sensitivity is the decimal `options.txt` value, not the in-game percent** — snapping to the percent would invent an error MC does not have (`mc` lands on exactly 1)
- `mul_decimals` is the SCOPE/ADS field's precision, deliberately separate from `decimals` even where they match — different input boxes, so folding them breaks the day one game changes only one
- Side fields: `mc.percent` (200), `rb.grid` (5), `r6.yaw_unit` (.02, the `MouseSensitivityMultiplierUnit` factor)

**Three `round_to(x, 2)` calls in `update_game_sens()` stay literal on purpose** — `bdo_hipfire_exact`, `sa_hipfire_exact`, `mc_hipfire`. Reference readouts, NOT game input grids (BDO/SA accept only integers so the exact value is unreachable; MC's percent is a derived label). Hoisting them into `constants.sens.<game>` would assert they are game facts.

## calc_sens_snap
**`calc_sens_snap(sens_name, ads_fov, width, height, cpi_scale, pubg_fov)` reproduces the ROUNDING every card value carries.** The card tells the player to type a value its field can hold, but `calc_rad_per_px` is exact — without this the trainer runs at a sens it never advised. Returns `yaw(typed) / yaw(ideal)`, folded into `state.camera.sens_scale` by `logic.update_camera_view()`. Computed on the GAME-resolution basis (`state.game.width / cpi_scale`), never the canvas.

Each branch rounds the way `update_game_sens()` displays it, then applies THAT GAME'S curve — linear games are `typed / exact`, but `bdo`/`sa` are affine, `mc` cubic, `pubg` `2 ** ((typed - exact) / step)`, `r6` snaps `yaw_unit` (`hipfire * .02`) at 6 dp not the sens. **`typed / exact` on the affine games is WRONG — their offset does not cancel.**

**`ads_fov` selects the FIELD CHAIN, not just a FOV** — a scoped view is a SECOND typed field on its own grid, and for `cs2`/`rb`/`val` the caller passes `constants.fov.<game>.ads_ref[scope]`, the fov that one SHARED field was matched at, not the fov the run renders. It was a `boolean ads` until 2026-08; now it is the SELECTED ADS fov, with **`0` meaning hipfire**, so `if (!ads_fov)` is the old `if (!ads)` and every branch reads the fov off the argument instead of a hardcoded `constants.fov.<game>.<scope>`. That is the whole reason one branch per game covers all of `state.game.ads_scope`'s options: the ROUNDING MODEL is uniform across a game's scopes, only the fov moves. Each branch still models whatever the card told the player to type: `al`/`cs2`/`val` a bare multiplier at `mul_decimals`, `ow` the hero PERCENT (through `calc_real_hfov(scopes.axis, ads_fov, …)`, since the heroes are raw vertical), `fn` NOTHING past the hipfire percent (Standard pins both zoom fields at 100 %, so its ADS half is the typed hipfire measured against the ideal at the scoped view), `pubg` its own absolute log sens, `r6` the shared `ads_unit` int, `rb` the whole Rivals chain as `calc_sens_rb_fields()` chose it (`effective` for hipfire, `× While Aiming / 100 × ads_fov / base` for a scope).
- **For multiplier games the ADS snap REPLACES the hipfire snap, not compounds** — `update_game_sens()` divides by the ALREADY-SNAPPED hipfire, so that rounding is absorbed and `typed_mul / exact_mul` is the whole answer
- `r6` is the exception that DOES compound: its file ratios divide by the UNROUNDED hipfire, so the `yaw_unit` snap survives. Its ADS half is now `round(mul / ads_unit) * ads_unit / mul` — the same arithmetic `calc_sens_r6_file()` does to build `x1`…`x12`, written out generically so it works for any scope instead of reading back the `x2_5` field
- `bdo` (no zoom-sens field), `lol`, `mc`, `sa` (forced TRG multipliers, applied separately) ignore `ads_fov`
- **`fn`'s ADS half carries no rounding at all and is still the second-largest miss on the board** (@FHD −0.952 % at `ADS(65)` down to −1.279 % at `Scoped(15)`; @21:9 −0.581 % to −0.909 %). It DEEPENS as the view narrows, and the two scope FOVs land within 0.02 pt of each other, because the gap is a property of the curve, not of any one scope. Zoom Scaling **Standard** scales yaw by a pure `tan(fov/2)` ratio while the ideal is the match-averaged `calc_rad_per_px`, and the two only agree at the hipfire FOV — with both percent fields pinned at 100 % the player has nothing left to type, so the trainer reproduces the gap instead of rounding to it. Do not "fix" it toward 0
- Adding a game means writing BOTH halves — an ADS branch reusing the hipfire snap silently runs the scope at a sens the card never showed
- **A VIEW THE TYPED FIELD WAS NOT MATCHED TO is NOT expressible here and must not be forced in.** `calc_sens_snap` answers "what did the player type", which is ONE field; when the run renders something that field was not matched to — a second zoom stage (`cs2`'s AWP/Automatic, `val`'s Operator x5) or a weapon sharing the field with another (`cs2`'s AUG, `rb`'s five non-rifle groups, `val`'s Guardian/Marshal/Spectre) — `logic.js` multiplies the result by `calc_sens_<game>(typed) / calc_sens_<game>(shown)`, one expression for both. Keeping it out means this function never needs a second fov argument.
  - **The two halves split cleanly by what moves them: that multiplier is ASPECT-ONLY, and `calc_sens_snap` is the ONLY resolution-sensitive term.** `calc_rad_per_px` is exactly ∝ `1/width`, so `width` cancels out of the ratio and the multiplier is bit-identical at 720p and 4K; only `calc_real_hfov` puts an aspect in it. Measured: `cs2` AUG **−1.07 %** at every 16:9 resolution, −0.83 % @21:9, −1.13 % @5:4, snap adding −0.02…−0.39 %; `rb` Slingshot **+8.44 %** @16:9, +36…37 % @21:9, **−12.87 %** @5:4, snap ±1.5 % (the only one crossing two 5 % grids); `val` Guardian **+4.54 % at EVERY aspect**, because `val.axis` is `"h"` so `calc_real_hfov` returns the raw fov and the ratio has no aspect term at all, leaving a ±0.05 % snap. So quote an ADS gap with its ASPECT, never its resolution — and keep both out of player-facing prose, which is why `tutorial.js` names only the direction and lets the HUD read the number out live
  - Second stages measured @FHD: **+3.75 % (AWP 40 → 10), +3.41 % (Automatic 40 → 15), +5.57 % (Operator 53.39 → 28.23)** — all three are the game handing you one box for two views, not a bug

## Per-game leftovers
- **`sa`'s TRG scope has NO settable zoom sens** — the engine forces `hipfire_yaw x0.25` at stage 1 (5×) and `x0.15` at stage 2 (15×), nowhere near monitor-matched: @FHD the matched ratios are `x0.2127` / `x0.0709`, so stage 2 runs **~111 % faster**. The trainer reproduces the forced values: the `sa` branch of `logic.update_camera_view()` writes `constants.fov.sa.x5_sens`/`x15_sens` into `sens_scale`, and `update_camera_projection()` just multiplies `sens` by it. `calc_sens_sa` still returns the hipfire in-game sens. Zoom FOVs are tan-ratio (`convert_deg_across_aspect(hipfire, 5, 1)`), not `hipfire / 5`
- **`bdo` is the only FPS game here that eats the OS pointer multiplier** — it reads processed cursor deltas, so yaw-per-raw-count is `engine(sens) × cpi_scale`. `calc_sens_bdo` takes `cpi_scale` and divides by it; `calc_cpi_bdo` multiplies by it. Both callers still pass the usual `width / cpi_scale` basis, so the two `cpi_scale`s cancel EXACTLY (`calc_rad_per_px` is ∝ `1/width`) and BDO's displayed sens does not move with the LOL/Windows sens — correct, since the reference feel and BDO's camera scale with the same multiplier. **Keep both factors written out**: folding them erases why BDO is exempt and breaks silently if `calc_rad_per_px` ever stops being ∝ `1/width`
- `lol_sens_to_cpi_scale()` is LOL-only — piecewise-linear over 11 anchors (`.031_25 … 3.5`) sampled every 10 sens. **LOL's slider is not an engine sens; it drives the WINDOWS POINTER SPEED**, so it is an OS multiplier, not a turn rate — that is why it writes `state.game.cpi_scale` and why every other card recomputes when it moves, and why the CPI normalizer rescales CPI for LOL instead of solving a sens. **`sens 50` hits `points[5]` = EXACTLY `1`** — the neutral notch, the only value that neither up- nor down-scales raw counts. That is the whole basis of the card's advice to leave it at 50 and tune CPI, so do not reshape the anchors without checking that index 5 stays 1. In-game the slider only moves in **5s** (`index.html#lol-sens` carries `grid="5"`); the anchors sit on the 10s, so odd multiples of 5 land halfway between two Windows notches by interpolation. LOL being a FLAT on-screen speed rather than a turn per inch is also why it is the reference the other games are matched against
- PUBG `game.pubg_fov` is `80` (fixed-FOV TPP hipfire) or `103` (FPP hipfire). Only the Normal/hipfire calc follows the preset — `FpsCameraFov` does not change the fixed ADS/scope FOVs, and `constants.fov.pubg.base` stays 80 as the REFERENCE those are derived from, whichever preset is selected. The INI rewrite writes the selected `FpsCameraFov`; `calc_cpi_pubg()` applies the same FOV to both yaw scaling and monitor matching. The old `calc_pubg_fpp_fov()` bisection double-corrected for parallax that cancels — don't resurrect it

## When you change…
- A sens formula: verify the matching `logic.update_camera_view()` branch — the FOV the renderer uses must match the FOV the calc assumes, or mouse and target scale diverge
- `calc_rad_per_px`: readers go through the `state.camera.sens` cache, so changes propagate automatically
- PUBG math: `calc_sens_pubg()` + `calc_pubg_converted()` + the INI rewrite in `controller/game_sens.js`, which writes BOTH `Sensitivity` and `LastConvertedSensitivity`
- Add a sens game: root `CLAUDE.md:Change map`
